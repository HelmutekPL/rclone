Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

What this application is

A private, single-user OAuth client used exclusively by its owner for personal backups to their own Google Drive via rclone.

Data collected

None. The application collects, stores and processes no personal data from any third party. It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, limited to the scopes granted by the owner during OAuth authorization (drive scope, as configured in rclone).

Data storage and sharing

No data is stored on any server operated by the application owner beyond the owner's own infrastructure. No data is shared with anyone.
